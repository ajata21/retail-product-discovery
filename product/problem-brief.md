# Problem brief: Exact-variant pickup clarity

October 20, 2026 — provisional desktop-web portfolio focus; not a PRD, solution specification, or validated customer priority.

The shopper's task is to compare men's running shoes in US size 10, within a maximum displayed price of $150, for pickup at the chosen Dallas store near ZIP 75231. To make a suitable choice, the shopper needs to understand whether pickup applies to the pictured shoe color, selected size, and chosen store together. A store-specific listing label can leave that relationship ambiguous when the product page offers pickup somewhere else. The proposed focus is this eligibility clarity, without assuming a particular interface solution.

The [completed Academy follow-up](../research/academy-website-followup.md#product-outcomes) documents a fixed selection of the first three filtered Best Match cards: Nike Air Max Invigor, adidas Swift Run 1.0, and Reebok Viva Speed. No products were replaced after outcomes were seen. Each card said pickup at Dallas, with nearby-store boxes unchecked. Nike Grey/Green, size 10, D, at $94.99 opened with nearby Lewisville pickup and Dallas email notification; the supplied browser-state JSON explicitly labels Dallas out of stock. The follow-up Nike screenshot shows only the upper product panel, not pickup details. These are observations attributed to the supplied ChatGPT Work session; local attachment inspection is not independent live-site verification.

The Nike observation repeats the [earlier pilot](../research/online-research-pilot.md#controlled-academy-website-check), including the same product and session environment. It is not another independent customer account or evidence of widespread failure. Successful comparisons matter: adidas Black, size 10, D, $79.99 and Reebok White/Navy, size 10, D, $49.99 displayed Dallas pickup eligibility. They show that the listing-to-product relationship can be consistent within this sample. Actual stock, order acceptance, payment, and fulfillment were not tested. All three prices met the displayed budget and all three transferred size 10, providing counterexamples to mandatory size reselection in these journeys.

Pickup clarity is the provisional desktop-web focus because a specific eligibility ambiguity was observed on the intended surface, alongside successful comparisons. Browsing-progress preservation remains an emerging alternative in the [pilot screening](../research/online-research-pilot.md#synthesis-and-next-action), with different app/navigation/inactivity triggers. In the [follow-up Back checks](../research/academy-website-followup.md#browser-back-outcomes), all three journeys retained filters and nearby-store exclusions; two shifted by 113 CSS pixels and none reset to the top. These short first-row desktop journeys did not test native apps, deep scrolling, inactivity, or cart/Home/Shop exits. They do not disprove the app complaints or automatically resolve original C3. This is a reasoned portfolio scope choice, not a measured ranking of customer needs.

Scope is pre-purchase desktop-web eligibility communication for the pictured variant, selected size, and chosen store. It excludes backend diagnosis, inventory accuracy, fulfillment operations, and app redesign. The working assumption is that understanding this relationship matters to the stated task; customer interpretation, priority, confusion, prevalence, severity, urgency, and business impact remain unmeasured. Filter matching rules, stock timing, other products/stores, and access to reliable exact-variant data remain unresolved. Historical evidence and [feedback qualifications](../research/customer-feedback-analysis.md) remain intact; no feedback accounts or accepted totals change.

A proposed future validation question is: can people correctly identify where the pictured shoe in their selected size is eligible for pickup, and explain the evidence they used? Any later comparison of current and alternative presentations would need its own design. No participant testing, usability result, or solution validation has been completed here.
