### Standing methodological limits

This is a bounded, self-selected public-source sample, not representative research or statistical saturation. Indexing, ranking, platform demographics, moderation, access, retailer size, and app/web differences can shape coverage. Claims may be unverifiable; ratings/likes do not establish reliability or prevalence. No interviews, controlled usability tests, verified stock records, or actual fulfillment measurements are provided by this method. Absence of relevant feedback is not evidence of no problem.

Initial two-hour-limit compliance and screening sequence are unverified; do not reconstruct them. A-01 follow-up has a separate maximum 90-minute budget; disclose actual stop position and gaps without filling them using irrelevant material. Public feedback cannot resolve the audit's Academy Dallas-only store-scope qualification or REI Arahi displayed-color/URL discrepancy retroactively. Retailer product counts remain non-equivalent measurements. Preserve uncertainty and separate historical walkthrough evidence from newly collected accounts.

Respect public-access restrictions and the protocol's privacy rules: no private data, author contact, bypassing access controls, unnecessary identifiers, or sensitive accounts unsuitable for publication. URLs may still identify authors; record this residual limitation.
